    .IO
    Route beat-synchronised pulses into demand nodes to score. The whole board pulses in unison — read the β cost, win the corridor.

    SETTINGS

    HOW TO PLAY

    • ◆You start as a source (the diamond). Drag from it to etch a trace across the lattice.
    • →Every source fires on beat 0 of each measure. Route your pulse into a □ node to capture it — it then pays you every measure.
    • βThe number at the head of your drag is the path's cost in beats. Diagonals cost 1.42. That number is the whole game.
    • ⌫Drag back over the path you're drawing to rub it out. WASD / arrows pan the camera.
    • ⚡Score to arm attacks. At 120, hover a cell you own and press Space to Surge (fry adjacent rival traces). At 400, aim at an enemy junction and press V to blow their via. At 900, hover an empty cell and press G to plant a permanent Jammer. Attacks cost score and have a cooldown.
    • •••The 8 pips top-left are the measure clock, shared by everyone. SYNC must stay green.
