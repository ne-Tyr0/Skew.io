    .IO
    Draw wires from your diamond to the glowing squares. Each square you reach pays you points every few seconds — grab and hold the most to win.

    SETTINGS

    HOW TO PLAY

    Goal: draw wires from your diamond to the glowing squares. Reaching a square captures it, and it pays you points every few seconds. Whoever has the most points is winning.

    • ◆The diamond is your home base. Click and drag from it to draw a wire across the grid.
    • →Your base fires a spark down your wires on the beat (the dots up top count the beat). Steer a spark into a □ glowing square to capture it.
    • ★A captured square keeps paying you points until a rival's spark reaches it. Grab and hold as many squares as you can.
    • ⌫Drag back over a wire to erase it. WASD / arrows move the view, scroll zooms, and the mini-map (bottom-right) jumps you anywhere.
    • ⚡As your score climbs you unlock attacks to wreck rivals' wires. The chips along the bottom show each one, its key, and when it's ready.
